About NorCal Valuations

NorCal Valuations provides professional manufactured home appraisal services throughout the Bay Area and Northern California. With over 10 years of experience in the appraisal field, we bring accuracy, professionalism, and industry knowledge to every valuation assignment.

Trusted by Lending Professionals

NorCal Valuations has been utilized by multiple banks and lending institutions for manufactured home appraisal assignments. That experience means our reports are prepared with the level of detail, clarity, and compliance expected by financial professionals, while also remaining accessible to private clients.

Serving Private Clients

In addition to lending-related appraisal work, we also assist private clients who need valuations for estates, probate, divorce proceedings, trusts, private sales, and personal financial decisions. Our goal is to make the appraisal process clear, professional, and straightforward from request to completed report.

Meet Cristina Bautista

NorCal Valuations is led by a licenced appraiser with over a decade of experience in the field, specializing in manufactured home valuations. With a strong background working with banks and lending institutions, she brings a high level of professionalism, accuracy, and reliability to every appraisal.

Her experience across both lending and private appraisal work ensures that each report meets industry standards while also addressing the specific needs of individual clients. Whether assisting with estate matters, legal proceedings, or financing requirements, she is committed to providing clear, dependable valuations.
